## Platform

web

## Stack

Angular (CLI 21 available locally) with GSAP for motion, including ScrollTrigger for scroll-driven scenes and parallax. The user chose this explicitly. They also asked for smooth scrolling, so add a smooth-scroll layer (e.g. Lenis) synced to ScrollTrigger. Deploy target: undecided.

## Users

All three buyer groups matter. No single audience comes first:

- **Overseas importers and distributors** buying coconuts in bulk (container loads) for their markets.
- **Domestic traders and wholesalers** in India.
- **Retail and supermarket chains** that need consistent grading, packing, and supply.

All three are B2B buyers judging one thing: can this exporter reliably supply good coconuts at volume, anywhere in the world? They usually arrive from a referral, a trade directory, or a search. They scan the page, then decide whether to send an enquiry.

## Product Purpose

The website for **SAKSAVI Agro Exports**, a coconut export business. It tells the supply story from grove to global delivery, shows the product range, and turns interested buyers into enquiries. Success means a qualified buyer sends an enquiry (product, quantity, destination).

## Positioning

"Pure Coconuts • Global Reach" (from the brand logo). SAKSAVI controls the whole chain, from palm to harvest to collection to shipment worldwide. The site should make that chain visible as one continuous story, not just claim it. Specific differentiators (origin region, grading standard, capacity) are not confirmed yet.

## Operating Context

The supply chain the story follows, as the user described it: coconuts grow well on the palm → mature coconuts fall and are harvested → a truck comes and collects them → they ship to destinations around the world. Buyers will mostly view the site on desktop, with some on mobile (often via WhatsApp links). This is assumed, not confirmed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product range (confirmed): semi-husked coconuts, fully husked/dehusked coconuts, tender coconuts, and processed products (copra, coconut oil, desiccated coconut).
- Primary action: an enquiry / request-a-quote. The channel is undecided: form, email, or WhatsApp.
- Not decided yet: farm or origin region, company address and contacts, minimum order quantities, packaging specs, Incoterms, and pricing. Leave these as clearly marked placeholders.
- Motion must stay smooth on mid-range laptops and phones. That is an explicit user requirement.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. There are no certifications, no destination-country list, no farm or warehouse photos, and no testimonials, volumes, or export history. Never invent any of these. Where the layout needs them, use clearly labelled placeholders that the client will fill in.

## Product Principles

1. **Show the chain, don't claim it.** The grove-to-world journey is the argument. Every scene should make supply reliability easier to believe.
2. **A buyer is always one step from an enquiry.** Storytelling must never trap someone who has already decided.
3. **Honest before impressive.** Unverified facts stay as placeholders. Credibility is the product for an exporter.
4. **Smoothness is part of the brand.** Janky motion reads as an unreliable supplier. Cut an effect before shipping it rough.
