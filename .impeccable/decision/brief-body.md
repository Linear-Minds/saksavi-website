## Scope

SAKSAVI Agro Exports landing page (single long scroll page, Angular). Mode: Persuade.

## Audience, job, action

B2B coconut buyers (overseas importers, Indian traders, retail chains) deciding whether this exporter can reliably supply. Action: send a quote request (product, quantity, port of discharge, contact). The working form appears in the first viewport and again at the close. Proof: none confirmed. Countries, certifications, capacity and origin stay as labelled placeholders.

## Memorable moment

The user's pinned story is told as scroll-scrubbed illustration: a palm grows heavy with coconuts → ripe coconuts fall → a truck arrives, collects them and drives off → it reaches the port, and container routes arc out across the globe. A consignment slip fills in alongside it, and a violet "SHIPPED ON BOARD" stamp lands at the end.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the shipment's own paperwork. Every scroll fills a numbered field, and the enquiry is the consignment form. It refuses the exporter default of a split hero with stock photo, a product-card row and a contact page.

OWN-WORLD: Deep palm green (#173D24) drenched ground. Pale security-print green (#DCE7D3) for document panels, with guilloche linework. Coconut-husk brown for trunks and nuts. Violet rubber-stamp ink (#5A3F8F) reserved strictly for confirmed states. Hairline document rules and numbered boxes "(1) Shipper". Wide/black Archivo for display, condensed Archivo for labels, Courier Prime for typed fill-ins. Square corners and no shadows: the paper sits flat.

STORY: The visitor understands what SAKSAVI ships, sees the whole palm-to-port chain run in one continuous scene, believes one exporter controls it, and sends a consignment enquiry.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed illustrated grove at dusk with parallax layers. The hero palm sits right of centre, its crown heavy with coconuts and the centre kept clear. A document header strip across the top doubles as nav (Shipper / Goods / Journey / Destination / Request quote). Box (5) at top-left holds the 96px wide-black headline "Pure coconuts. Global reach." and typed subcopy. A working "Shipping instruction" form sits bottom-right (product, quantity, port, contact, submit). A stage strip along the bottom reads Grown / Harvested / Collected / Shipped, with a route line and a "now" marker.

FORM: Bill of Lading, candidate 7 on my resonance list; seed: 31c918cf0cf43af5. Signature interaction: a pinned scroll-scrubbed GSAP timeline (grow → fall → truck collect → route to globe) drives the stage strip, typewriter-filled fields and the final violet stamp. Raises: one unbroken route line with a single "now" marker; scale-only hierarchy (tiny labels, huge values); prior stage ghosted while the new stage arrives bright; one transformation per phone viewport; violet only for confirmed states; centre kept clear for the subject.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
